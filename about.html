About Gengmeng Data & Intelligence

I didn't expect a quantitative finance internship to change the way I think, but it did.

Building investment models showed me how easily an assumption could be wrong. I realized that I liked being proven wrong, because rethinking the mistake helped me improve the logic and move closer to a better answer.

I carried that mindset into my internship working with marketing data. Every client and campaign behaved differently. I liked noticing changes in the numbers and asking what was driving them, then using the answer to help clients move closer to their goals.

That curiosity does not stop when I close my laptop. It is probably why kite surfing is next on my list. I can also easily lose track of time in an art museum. My interest in art is why I enjoy using simple visuals and thoughtful design to tell the story behind the numbers, helping people focus on what matters rather than showing every detail.

I also laugh a lot. I take my work seriously, but not myself. The best teams I have been part of were open-minded. People could ask honest questions, challenge ideas, and still enjoy solving the problem together.

If any of this resonates with you, I'd love to connect. Whether you'd like to discuss a project, exchange ideas, or simply say hello, feel free to reach out via LinkedIn or email.
